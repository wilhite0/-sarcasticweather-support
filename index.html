Plans and billing

In SarcasticWeather 1.0.2, you can choose from three plans.

Basic (Free) includes current weather, hourly and 10-day forecasts, observed radar, official U.S. alerts where available, the interactive Timeline, a small Current Weather widget, still forecast cards and a selection of quips, Sun & Moon encounters and spoken weather commentary.

Pro includes everything in Basic, plus the full quip library, more personality controls and voice modes, all Sun & Moon battles and UFO encounters, animated forecast cards, and more Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

Premium includes everything in Pro, plus Cinematic Timeline, supported weather Live Activities and Dynamic Island presentations on compatible iPhones, and Apple Watch forecasts, complications and Smart Stack widgets.

Pro and Premium each offer a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription. Both billing periods unlock the same features within a tier. Apple shows the price in your currency before you confirm. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. To avoid the next renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You keep access through the period you paid for.

In SarcasticWeather, open Settings and choose Manage subscription to change or cancel your plan. Choose Restore Purchases to restore eligible purchases, including earlier lifetime purchases, using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Privacy Policy

Updated November 21, 2026

SarcasticWeather is designed to provide weather information while minimizing the collection of personal information.

Location

You can select a city manually or allow SarcasticWeather to use your current precise or approximate location. The app uses the selected location for weather, alerts, radar and astronomy. You can change the city in the app or withdraw location permission in Settings under Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Weather and third-party services

Apple supplies forecasts and location search. NOAA and the National Weather Service supply observed radar and U.S. alerts. The U.S. Naval Observatory supplies rise, set and lunar information. Depending on the feature, requests include your city search, coordinates, map area, date or time zone. Like other internet services, these providers receive network information needed to answer the request. SarcasticWeather uses location for app functionality and does not link it to your identity or use it for tracking.

Storage, sharing and deletion

The app keeps the selected location, recent forecasts, preferences and information about verified access on your device. Cached weather is refreshed or replaced as new readings arrive. Widgets use shared local data, and the iPhone can send weather, preferences and access status to your paired Apple Watch. Forecast cards go to the recipient or service you choose in the share sheet.

To remove local forecast data and preferences, delete the app on the devices where you use it. Apple maintains its purchase history separately. Copies of forecast cards you shared are controlled by their recipients. To ask about or request deletion of information you sent to support, email the contact below.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. SarcasticWeather does not receive or store your payment card information.

Information We Do Not Sell

SarcasticWeather does not sell your personal information.
